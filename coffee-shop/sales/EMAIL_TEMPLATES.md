# Email templates — Nova App Development shop outreach

How to use these: send from your normal inbox, ONE shop at a time, and
always swap in the [bracketed] bits before hitting send. Do not bulk-send
or use automation tools — a few dozen identical emails gets your address
flagged as spam, and a personal line about THEIR shop is what gets replies.
Attach `ONE_PAGER.pdf` to Template 1.

Log every send in `LEAD_TRACKER.csv` (Touch columns) so nothing slips.

## Deliverability rules (why we don't automate)

- Send individually from your own inbox. Never CC/BCC a list of shops.
- 10–15 outreach emails a day maximum from one address.
- Always personalize the first line — it's also what Gmail's spam filter
  and the OWNER both notice first.
- A branded address (brayden@novaappdev.com or similar via a custom
  domain + Google Workspace, ~$6/mo) replies better than @yahoo.com —
  worth setting up before heavy outreach. Keep using Yahoo until then;
  never switch addresses mid-conversation with a lead.
