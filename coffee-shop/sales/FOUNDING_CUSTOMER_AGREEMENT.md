# Founding Customer Agreement (simple letter of intent)

_A one-page sign-up sheet for tomorrow's pitches. It's deliberately simple and
low-pressure — the goal is a real commitment, not a scary contract. **This is a
starting draft, not legal advice**; have a lawyer glance at it before you rely
on it heavily (many will review a one-pager for ~$100–200)._

## What [Company Name] will deliver

A branded mobile ordering app for the shop named above, including:

- Customer app (web + Android at launch; iOS following) with the shop's name,
  logo, colors, and full menu
- Order-ahead with payment by card/Apple Pay/Google Pay via Stripe — payouts go
  directly to the shop's own bank account; [Company Name] never holds funds and
  takes 0% of sales
- A rewards program with rules chosen by the shop (points per dollar, reward
  tiers, special deals)
- An order dashboard that runs on the shop's existing tablet or phone
- Setup done for you: menu entry, branding, staff training, launch signage
  (QR table tents + register sign)

**Target launch:** within [6] weeks of signing, subject to the shop providing
menu details and completing Stripe's ~10-minute payment onboarding.

## Founding terms (first 5 shops)

- **$0 setup — no build fee** and **$312/month, locked for 12 months**
  (the base rate may rise for later shops; founding shops keep this rate
  for their first year)
- Billing starts **only when the app goes live**, not today
- **No long-term contract** — cancel anytime with 30 days' notice
- Card processing fees (Stripe's standard ~2.9% + 30¢) are paid by the shop,
  the same as any card terminal
- If the app is not live within [10] weeks of signing, this agreement is void
  and any deposit is refunded in full

## Reserving a founding spot

- [ ] **Reserve with a $100 deposit** (applied to the first month; fully
  refundable any time before launch), **or**
- [ ] **Reserve with signature only** (spot held for 14 days)

The shop agrees to: provide its menu and logo, complete Stripe onboarding,
keep the order tablet at the counter during open hours, and give honest
feedback as a founding partner.
