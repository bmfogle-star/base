# Remote Demo Script — Zoom (live) or Loom (recorded)

_Target length: under 3 minutes. Energy up, no feature tours — show the loop,
name the money, make the offer._

## Screen setup (2 minutes, before you record/call)

- Open **two browser windows side by side**:
  - LEFT: the customer app — `https://base-production-175d.up.railway.app`
    (make the window narrow; the app is phone-width and looks like a phone)
  - RIGHT: the dashboard — same URL + `/dashboard`, logged in with your PIN
- Turn your sound ON — the dashboard chime landing on mic is a great moment.
- Have the QR code image handy to paste in Zoom chat (or link in Loom notes).

## The script

**[0:00 — Hook, on camera or over the app]**
> "Your regulars already use an app like this every day — it's just
> Starbucks'. I build the same thing for independent shops: your name, your
> logo, your menu. Let me show you the whole thing in two minutes."

**[0:15 — Customer side, LEFT window]**
> "This is a demo shop. Here's what your customer sees — your menu, their
> star balance, this week's deals."
Order something with personality: latte → oat milk → extra shot → checkout.
> "They pay right here — card or Apple Pay — pick a pickup time, done."

**[0:50 — THE MOMENT, RIGHT window]**
Let the chime play.
> "That sound is your counter. Any tablet or phone you already own — no new
> hardware. One tap: preparing…"
Tap **Start preparing**, then **Mark ready**, then point at LEFT window:
> "…and their phone just updated. They walk in, grab the cup, no line."

**[1:20 — Rewards, LEFT window]**
Open the Rewards tab.
> "Every dollar earns stars — you set the rules. Free drip at 50, free
> pastry at 100, whatever fits your margins. It's the punch card your
> customers can't lose — and unlike the punch card, you see who your top 20
> customers actually are."
(Flash the dashboard's Customers tab for two seconds.)

**[1:50 — The money slide, just say it straight]**
> "The part every owner asks about: payments run through Stripe — the money
> goes straight from your customer to YOUR bank account, same daily deposit
> rhythm as your card machine. I never touch it, and I take zero percent of
> your sales. Flat $312 a month, no setup or build fee at all — my first five
> shops lock that rate for a year — and billing doesn't even start until
> you're live. Two or three extra orders a day pays for it."

**[2:20 — Close]**
LIVE ZOOM: paste the app link in chat →
> "Open that on your phone right now and order something — I'll wait."
(Their phone buzzing IS the close. Then:) "Want me to build this with your
name on it? I'll lock one of the five founding spots for you today —
signature holds it, nothing due until launch."

LOOM: end card / final line →
> "If you want this with your shop's name on it, reply to this email or grab
> 15 minutes with me — [your phone / calendar link]. I'm only taking five
> founding shops at this price."

## Objection quick-answers (keep under your keyboard)

- **"My customers aren't techy."** — "They order from Starbucks and DoorDash
  already. Scan, tap, pay — if they can text, they can use this."
- **"I already have Square/Toast."** — "Keep it. This runs alongside for
  order-ahead and loyalty; nothing about your register changes. Deeper POS
  integration is on my roadmap."
- **"What if it doesn't work for us?"** — "Cancel anytime, 30 days notice.
  And billing only starts when you're live. Your risk is a QR code on the
  counter."
- **"Why not just use [big platform]?"** — "They take a cut of every sale or
  bury you in a marketplace next to your competitors. I take 0% and your
  customer list is yours. And I'm local — when you call, I answer."

## Recording tips (Loom)

- One take is fine. A small stumble reads as human, not sloppy.
- Keep your face bubble ON — this is a trust sale.
- Name the shop in the first line of a custom intro when you can:
  "Maria — this is what a Beanhouse app would look like." (Even with the
  Bluebird demo, personalizing the first sentence doubles reply rates.)
- Subject line that works: **"Your own app — 2 min demo (no pitch deck)"**
