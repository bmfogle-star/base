7 · Stripe — how your app takes payments (10 min)

Stripe deposits your app sales straight into YOUR bank account daily, like your card machine. We never touch your money and take 0% of sales.

  1. Go to stripe.com → Sign up. Use your business email.
  2. Complete "Activate your account": business info, EIN or SSN, and the bank account where deposits should land. (~10 min; approval usually same-day.)
  3. Then give us access one of these two ways:

Option A — invite us (recommended, most secure)

In Stripe: click the gear icon (Settings) → Team → + New member → enter ________________@____________ (we'll give you our email) → role: Developer → Invite. Done — we securely retrieve what we need, you can remove us anytime.

Option B — send the key via self-destructing link

In Stripe: Developers → API keys → Secret key → Reveal → copy the code starting with sk_live_. Then go to onetimesecret.com, paste the key, click "Create a secret link," and text/email us THE LINK. It self-destructs after we open it once.
Never put the key itself in a plain email or text — only the one-time link. Anyone with that key could act on your Stripe account.
