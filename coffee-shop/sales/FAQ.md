# FAQ — answers for coffee shop owners

_The questions owners actually ask, in the order they usually ask them.
Learn the bolded one-liners; the detail underneath is your backup._

### How much does it cost?
**"Flat $312 a month, zero setup fee — and I take zero percent of your sales. My first five shops lock that rate for a year."**
Two to three extra orders a day covers it. Card processing is Stripe's
standard ~2.9% + 30¢ — the same ballpark as any card terminal, paid to Stripe,
not to me.

### Do you take a cut of my sales?
**"No. Never. Your sales are yours — I charge a flat monthly fee, that's it."**
(This is your biggest differentiator vs. marketplace apps — say it early
and often.)

### Where does the money go when a customer pays?
**"Straight from your customer to your bank account. I never touch it."**
Payments run through Stripe under the shop's own account, with automatic
daily payouts — the same rhythm as their existing card machine.

### What equipment do I need to buy?
**"Nothing. Orders show up on any tablet or phone you already own."**
The dashboard is a web page: iPad, old Android tablet, even a phone propped
by the register. New orders arrive with a chime.

### How do my customers get the app?
**"They scan a QR code at your register or a table tent — it opens instantly,
and they can save it to their home screen like any app."**
No download wall, no app-store account needed. App Store / Play Store
listings are a later upgrade for shops that want them.

### Does it work with my Square / Toast / Clover?
**"It runs alongside your register — nothing about your current setup
changes. Staff see app orders on the tablet and ring life goes on as normal."**
Deeper POS integration (orders flowing straight into Square) is on the
roadmap once the first shops are live.

### How does the rewards program work? Who pays for the free drinks?
**"Customers earn stars per dollar; you decide what stars unlock, so you
control the cost."**
Default: 2★ per $1, free drip at 50★ (≈ a free coffee per ~$25 spent — about
a 10% loyalty cost, similar to a punch card, but you see exactly who your
best customers are and they come back more often). Every number is
adjustable from the dashboard.

### What about refunds and mistakes?
**"One click in your Stripe dashboard, money back on their card."**
The shop keeps full control; no approval from me needed.

### Is my customers' data safe? Do you sell it?
**"Card numbers never touch my system — Stripe handles all payment data on
their secure page. The app only keeps a first name, a phone number, and
order history — and it's never sold or shared. There's a plain-English
privacy policy right in the app."**
Also true and worth saying: the customer list belongs to the shop. If they
ever leave, they take it with them.

### What if the internet goes down / the app breaks?
**"Your register keeps working — the app is an extra lane, never a
dependency. If the app has an outage, customers just walk in like they do
today."**

### Am I locked into a contract?
**"No. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice, and billing doesn't even start
until your app is live."**

### Why not just use Joe Coffee / a marketplace app?
**"Marketplaces put you on a shelf next to your competitors and take ~10% of
every order. This is YOUR app — your brand, your customer list, 0% of sales."**

### Why you and not a big company?
**"Because when something's weird at 7am on a Tuesday, you text me and I
answer. I'm building this with five local founding shops, not five thousand
accounts."**

### How long until my shop is live?
**"A few weeks from signing. I need your menu, logo, and 10 minutes to set up
your Stripe account. Billing starts only when you're live."**

### What do you need from me to start?
**"A signature to hold a founding spot, then your menu and logo. That's it —
I do the rest."**
