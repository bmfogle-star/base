## Your customers already use an app like this every day. It's just not yours.

**Nova App Development builds your coffee shop its own app** — your name,
your logo, your menu — with the three things that keep regulars coming back:

- **Order ahead.** Customers order and pay from their phone, walk in, and
  skip the line. You hand them a cup, not a wait.
- **A rewards program you control.** Stars on every purchase — in the app
  and at the register, automatically. You set the rewards: free drip at 50
  stars, free pastry at 100, anything you like. No punch cards.
- **A direct line to your regulars.** Double-Star Tuesday, a birthday
  drink, a slow-afternoon push — you decide, it shows up on their phones.

## No new hardware. No new workflow.

- Works with the **Square register you already own**. App orders appear on
  your register like any other order; counter sales earn stars
  automatically; marking an order ready updates the customer's phone live.
- No app-store downloads — customers **scan a QR code at your counter**
  and your app is on their phone in one tap.
- Money goes **straight to your bank** via Stripe (same daily payout
  rhythm as your card machine). **We never hold your money.**
- **We take 0% of your sales.** Flat monthly price.
- Your customer list and data are **yours, not ours** — unlike marketplace
  apps that list you next to your competitors.
- We do the setup for you: menu, photos, branding, staff training. You
  keep making coffee.
