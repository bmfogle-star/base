The business in one paragraph

LocalBrew-Dev gives independent coffee shops their own branded mobile ordering and rewards app — the experience Starbucks customers get, for shops that could never afford to build it. Customers scan a QR code at the register, add the shop's app to their phone in one tap, order ahead, and earn stars they redeem for free drinks. The shop pays a flat monthly fee; we take zero percent of sales.

How a shop goes live (the machine we run)

  1. Sign — service agreement (DocuSign-ready). Billing does not start yet.
  2. Intake — owner fills one sheet: menu, logo, hours, reward rules (30–45 min, remote).
  3. Build — their branded app is assembled and tested in 3–5 days.
  4. Connect — their Stripe (payments to their bank) and Square (register link), guided remotely.
  5. Launch — staff training call, QR signs at the register, a real $3 test order placed and refunded while they watch. Billing starts this day.

Ground rules (protect the business)

Never put API keys or passwords in a text, email, or chat. Live keys go only into the hosting dashboard, and key handoffs use one-time self-destructing links (onetimesecret.com). If a key ever leaks, it gets rotated the same day.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication on every account tied to the business (email, GitHub, Stripe, Square, Railway, Facebook).
  • We are a software vendor, never the merchant of record — shop sales, sales tax, and refunds live in the shop's own Stripe/Square.
  • Customer data is deliberately minimal: first name, phone number, order history. No card numbers ever touch our servers (Stripe handles those).
  • Menu wording, prices, and allergy claims are the shop's responsibility — we publish what they provide.
