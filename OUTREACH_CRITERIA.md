# Coffee Shop Outreach Criteria

Target profile for Nova App Development cold outreach (the pitch: their own
branded mobile ordering + rewards app, demoed via this repo's app at
https://base-production-175d.up.railway.app).

## Must-haves

1. **Independent** — no chains. Blank Street, Blue Bottle, Starbucks etc. are
   the foil in the pitch ("they engineered loyalty with their apps"), never
   the target.
2. **Anywhere in the USA** — not limited to New York; any city or state.
3. **Strong third-party validation** — every email leads with a specific,
   verifiable proof point, e.g.:
   - Near-perfect Google rating with a high review count (e.g. 5.0 across 800
     reviews).
   - Press "best of" accolades (Food & Wine, CBS, local outlets).
4. **No mobile ordering / rewards app of their own** — this gap is the hook of
   every pitch. Verify before sending.

## Bonus signals (strengthen the pitch)

- Multiple locations with no shared rewards balance across them.
- A reputation for regulars and loyalty (e.g. staff greet customers by name)
  that an app could formalize.
